# Gamification

Gamification exists to reinforce learning and return behaviour, not to obstruct motivated students.

Initial concepts are XP, levels, İz (the learner-facing habit/streak concept), daily goals, quests, achievements, checkpoints, boss tests, and progress feedback. XP must remain an auditable activity measure separate from mastery. Hearts, energy, ads, alternative İz rules, and boss variants are experiments and must be feature-flagged before introduction.

No gamification logic exists yet. The İz counter and week strip, the XP and gem totals, the heart count, the completion statistics, and the quest board are all presentation-only preview values. Before implementation, product must decide İz timezone and grace rules, XP award rules, abuse tolerance, and whether any reward has competitive value.
