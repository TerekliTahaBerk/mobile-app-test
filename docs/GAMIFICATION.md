# Gamification

Gamification exists to reinforce learning and return behaviour, not to obstruct motivated students.

Initial concepts are XP, levels, streaks, daily goals, quests, achievements, checkpoints, boss tests, and progress feedback. XP must remain an auditable activity measure separate from mastery. Hearts, energy, ads, alternative streak rules, and boss variants are experiments and must be feature-flagged before introduction.

No gamification logic belongs in Milestone 1. Before implementation, product must decide streak timezone and grace rules, XP award rules, abuse tolerance, and whether any reward has competitive value.
